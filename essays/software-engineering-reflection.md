	This semester has taught me all about the fundamentals of software engineering. There were many concepts I learned, and among them I believe agile project management and ethics in software development are what stuck with me the most and have reformed my way of thinking about software engineering.

## How Agile Project Management is A Valuable Life Skill
Firstly, the way we used agile project management is through Issue Driven Project Management. This style breaks down the project into smaller tasks, much like a divide and conquer approach. Each task or issue will have a deadline and person/people assigned to it. This allows everyone to keep track of what is being done and schedule and what tasks might take longer than initially anticipated. Through this, people can jump in to help with the tasks that need to be done and eventually through all these small tasks, the large project will be completed. This way of project management is not only valuable in software engineering, but in life as a whole because it can be done in any project, individually or working with others.

## Ethics of Software Engineering
There are eight principles for the ethics of software engineering, and while I will not go in depth through them all, they are as listed: public, client and employer, product, judgement, management, profession, colleagues, and self. These are important for any software engineer because it is important to make sure that any design has the interest of all of these principles in mind. The biggest one being the public, as it is important to make sure any design made adheres to the public’s best interest. These ethics are important because they aim to ensure the well-being and safety of everyone involved, maintain the trust of users of future products, and guide professionals to act responsibly. Overall, it is important and I will always look back on these principles when attempting to develop a new product.

## My Learnings Overall
ICS 314 has taught me many valuable fundamentals. Some of these not only apply in class, but in life as well. This is especially true with the two fundamentals expanded on above, agile project management and ethics of software engineering. These are of course not the only fundamentals learned in the course, as there are many important lessons learned, such as coding standards and design patterns and I believe these are all beneficial to any software engineer.
