EEGToolkit is a MATLAB-based data preprocessing toolkit that provides a streamlined preprocessing pipeline for EEG data within the EEGLAB environment. It integrates widely used EEGLAB plugins and automates common preprocessing steps such as filtering, artifact removal, and IC classification.
